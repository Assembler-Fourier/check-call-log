Tonight's check calls, made from the roster

This is Check Call Log's own Python code, running in your browser (through Pyodide) on a made-up roster for "Northwind Security". Pick a night and a controller: it works out everyone who needs a check call during that shift and when, plus the patrol log and the shift log, and prints all three like the paper forms in the binder. Nothing leaves your browser.

Who to call, and when

Everyone whose shift starts during the controller's, called about an hour before they start, in time order - with the odd cases spotted: a name with no time, a venue with nobody on it, a start after midnight.

Three forms, one click

The check call log, the campus patrol log and the shift log, written as one PDF laid out like the paper forms - by the tool itself, no Word or Excel needed.

In the desktop app as well

Ticking a call turns the guard's name green on the roster, a WhatsApp reader fills in who replied, the window hands over to the next controller 15 minutes early, and every change can be undone. Those need Windows and a real roster, so they are not on this page.

Everything shown is invented: Northwind Security, its controllers, guards and venues. The roster is rebuilt in your browser so its newest week is always this week.
